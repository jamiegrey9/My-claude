In practice

Schematic, not a service drawing. The chamber proportions follow the published CRC-55tR figures: about 17 cm across and 44 cm tall, with a 6.1 cm × 25.4 cm well and a 12 atm ultra-pure argon fill. The electrode layout, board arrangement and screen contents are simplified illustrations. Ion drift is slowed by many orders of magnitude so you can see it, and the screen reading is an example.
